Workspace comes on the second level of hierarchy after **Team**. It's a place where you can create collections related to a single entity.

For example, one Product team can have an Engineering Wiki, Product Doc, Sprint planning, Marketing,  and other **workspaces** belonging to the team. 

**Workspace** can have a **collection of pages** that users can add content to and these collections could even have nested sub-collections, creating a hierarchical structure.
