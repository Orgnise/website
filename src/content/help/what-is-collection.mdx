Collections are the way of organising content into meaningful categories. Think of them like different folders where you can store your work.

Pages are stored in **collection** – **collections** where you can collaborate on work and keep all your content organised. It’s best to group related content together in the same collection, but you can create as many or as few **collection** as your team needs. 

For example, one marketing team might keep all of its work in one collection, with a page for each campaign, while another might set up a separate collection for every single campaign. Each space comes with an overview (or homepage) and a blog, so it’s easy to share updates and announcements with your whole team.
