We are committed to providing you with a seamless experience on our platform. To ensure you have complete control over your personal information, we have introduced features that allow you to update your account details and delete your account if you choose to no longer use our services.

### **Update Your User Info**

We understand the importance of keeping your account information up-to-date. Therefore, we have made it simple for you to edit your details whenever necessary. Here's how you can manage your account information:

1. **Access Account Settings:** Navigate to your account settings to make changes.
2. **Edit Personal Details:** Update your name, email address, and password as needed.
3. **Save Changes:** Any modifications you make will be securely saved in our database.

Keeping your information current ensures you receive important notifications and updates tailored to you.

### **Delete Your Account**

If you decide to leave our platform, you can delete your account effortlessly. Follow these steps to permanently remove your account and associated data:

1. **Find the Delete Account Button:** Go to your account settings and locate the "Delete Account" button.
2. **Confirmation Prompt:** When you click on the "Delete Account" button, a confirmation message will appear to ensure you truly want to proceed.
3. **Ownership Verification:** If you are the owner of any existing teams, the system will prompt you to either delete or transfer ownership of the teams before proceeding. This step ensures that team data is managed appropriately.
4. **Permanent Deletion:** Once confirmed, all data associated with your account will be permanently deleted from our database.

We respect your decision to leave and ensure that your data is removed securely and completely.

### **Why These Features Matter**

Having the ability to update your account information and delete your account provides you with flexibility and control over your personal data. We prioritize your privacy and aim to make your experience as user-friendly as possible.

Should you have any questions or need assistance with managing your account, our support team is here to help. Thank you for being a valued user of our platform.
