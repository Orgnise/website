Added four new roles to the team, each with their own set of permissions to help and collaborate more effectively.

    **Owner**

    - Has full control over the team, including the ability to update the team name, slug, and description, delete the team, invite team members, create workspaces, update workspaces, delete workspaces, and upgrade the team plan.

    **Moderator**

    - Can invite team members, create workspaces, update workspaces, and delete workspaces. They are responsible for ensuring that the team's workflow runs smoothly and efficiently.

    **Member**

    - Don't have any permissions on the team level but Will have permissions on the workspace level, allowing them to contribute to the team's projects and collaborate with other members.

    **Guest**

    - Don't have any permissions on the team level but will also have permissions on the workspace level, allowing them to participate in specific projects or tasks.
