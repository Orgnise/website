We are excited to announce the addition of four new roles to our team structure, each designed with specific permissions to help streamline collaboration and improve workflow efficiency. Here’s a quick overview of the new roles:

### **Owner**

Owners have full control over the team, including the power to:

- Update the team name, slug, and description
- Delete the team
- Invite team members
- Create, update, and delete workspaces
- Upgrade the team plan

### **Moderator**

Moderators play a crucial role in maintaining team efficiency. They can:

- Invite team members
- Create, update, and delete workspaces

### **Member**

Members do not have permissions at the team level but are vital contributors at the workspace level. They can:

- Collaborate on team projects
- Contribute to specific tasks and initiatives

### **Guest**

Guests also lack team-level permissions but are granted access at the workspace level. They can:

- Participate in specific projects or tasks

These roles are designed to foster a more organized and collaborative environment, ensuring everyone has the appropriate level of access and responsibility. We believe this will significantly enhance team productivity and project management.

For any questions or assistance in assigning roles, please contact our support team. We look forward to seeing how these new roles will benefit your team!
