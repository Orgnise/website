A Workspace would have a collection of pages that users can add content to. These **collections** could even have nested sub-collections, creating a hierarchical structure so it's almost like a **wiki-style** setup, where the pages are the fundamental units that contain the actual knowledge and information.

And users can organize those pages into various collections and **subcollections** to keep things nice and tidy.

This gives teams a really flexible and intuitive way to build out their knowledge base, with the ability to nest and structure the content however works best for their needs.
