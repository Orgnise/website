A team is a collaborative space where you and your colleagues can work together seamlessly. Within a team, you can create workspaces, add items, and organize collections, all in one centralized hub.

## How to create a Team?

1. Click on the **Create Team** button

       className=" rounded-lg border border-gray-100"

2. Enter the **Team Name**
3. Enter the **Team Description**
