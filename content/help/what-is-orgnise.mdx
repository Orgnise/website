Orgnise is a platform to create a single source of truth for all important information, such as use-cases, wikis, tech documentation, company information, policies, guidelines, announcements, and more.
<Note variant="info">
  Fun fact: The name **Orgnise** comes from the words **Organize** and **Knowledge**. We believe that knowledge is the most important asset for any team, and we want to help you organize it in the best way possible.
</Note>

## What can I do with Orgnise?

If you are a marketer or a business owner, Orgnise has the perfect suite of features for you to better manage your knowledge base.

- **Team knowledge base**: Create a central repository of knowledge for your team to access and contribute to.
- **Customizable workspaces**: Create workspaces for different teams, projects, or topics to keep your knowledge organized.
- **Nested collections**: To organize your knowledge base in a way that makes sense to you.
- **Granular user permissions**: to control who can view, edit, or delete your knowledge base.
- **Robust search and filtering**: Quickly find the information you need with powerful search and filtering capabilities.
- **Rich text editor**: Create beautiful, easy-to-read articles with our built-in rich text editor.
- **Team collaboration**: Orgnise is built for teams. You can [invite your teammates](how-to-invite-teammates) to your account for free and collaborate on your knowledge base together.


## How is Orgnise different from other alternatives?
Orgnise is designed to be the most user-friendly knowledge base tool on the market. We've spent countless hours talking to our users and iterating on our product to make sure it's easy to use and intuitive. Here are a few things that set Orgnise apart from other solutions:
- **Simple and intuitive interface**: Orgnise is designed to be easy to use for everyone on your team, regardless of their technical expertise.
- **Powerful features**: Orgnise has all the features you need to manage your knowledge base effectively, without any unnecessary bloat.
- **Great customer support**: Our team is always here to help you with any questions or issues you might have. We pride ourselves on our fast response times and friendly service.
- **Affordable pricing**: Orgnise is priced competitively to make it accessible to teams of all sizes. We offer a free plan with generous limits, as well as paid plans with additional features and support.
- **Regular updates and improvements**: We're constantly working to make Orgnise better for our users. We release new features and improvements regularly based on user feedback.
- **Privacy and security**: We take the privacy and security of your data seriously. Orgnise is built with industry-standard security practices to keep your information safe.
