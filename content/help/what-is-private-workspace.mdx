## The Power of Private Workspaces: Collaboration with Control

In today's fast-paced work environment, teams need a way to collaborate effectively while also maintaining control over sensitive information. This is where private workspaces come in – a powerful tool that allows teams to work together while keeping certain information private. In this article, we'll explore the benefits and features of private workspaces, and how they can help your team work more efficiently.

## What is a Private Workspace?

A private workspace is a workspace that is only visible to selected team members. This means that only those who have been explicitly invited to join the workspace can see it and access its contents. This level of control is essential for teams working on sensitive projects or dealing with confidential information.

## How Private Workspaces Work

If a team member has not been invited to join a private workspace, they will not see it in their workspace list. This ensures that only authorized team members can access the workspace and its contents. However, if a team member is invited to join a private workspace, it will appear in their workspace list with a 'lock' icon, indicating that it is a private workspace.

## Customizing Workspace Privacy

One of the key benefits of private workspaces is that you can change the workspace privacy settings in the workspace settings. This allows you to tailor your workspace to your team's specific needs, ensuring that the right people have access to the right information.
<Note>
  **Note:** You can only change the privacy settings of a workspace if you are the **Owner** or **Moderator** of the team or **editor** of the workspace.
</Note>

## Benefits of Private Workspaces

Private workspaces offer a range of benefits for teams, including:

* **Controlled access**: Only authorized team members can access the workspace and its contents.
* **Confidentiality**: Sensitive information remains private and secure.
* **Flexibility**: You can change the workspace privacy settings to suit your team's needs.

## Conclusion

Private workspaces are a powerful tool for teams looking to collaborate effectively while maintaining control over sensitive information. By providing a private and secure space for your team to work together, private workspaces can help boost productivity, improve communication, and drive results. With their flexible privacy settings and secure access, private workspaces are an essential tool for any team looking to succeed.
