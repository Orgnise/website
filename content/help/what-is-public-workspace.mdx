When working in a team, it's essential to have a shared space where everyone can collaborate and access important information. This is where public workspaces come in. In this article, we'll explore what public workspaces are, how they work, and what benefits they offer.

## What is a Public Workspace?

A public workspace is a shared area that is visible to your entire team. This means that every team member can join this workspace and access its contents. Whether you're working on a project, sharing files, or collaborating on a task, public workspaces provide a centralized hub for your team to come together.

## Accessibility and Privacy

One of the key benefits of public workspaces is that they are only accessible to your team. This means that only authorized team members can view and access the contents of the workspace. Your workspace is not publicly available on the internet, ensuring that sensitive information remains private and secure.

<Note>
 **Note:** If you need to share information with external partners or clients, you can invite them as a guest in your team. Guests have limited access to your workspaces and can only view the workspaces they are invited to.
 For more information on roles, see [Roles and Permissions](/help/article/roles-and-permission)
</Note>

## Customizing Workspace Privacy

But what if you need to adjust the privacy settings of your workspace? Fortunately, you can easily change the workspace privacy in the workspace settings. This allows you to tailor your workspace to your team's specific needs and ensure that the right people have access to the right information.
<Note>
  **Note:** You can only change the privacy settings of a workspace if you are the **Owner** or **Moderator** of the workspace.
</Note>
## Conclusion

Public workspaces are a powerful tool for teams looking to collaborate and work together more effectively. By providing a shared space for your team to access and share information, public workspaces can help boost productivity, improve communication, and drive results. With their flexible privacy settings and secure access, public workspaces are an essential tool for any team looking to succeed.
