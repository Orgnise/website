Workspace comes on the second level of hierarchy after **Team**. It's a place where you can create collections related to a single entity.

## How to create a Workspace?

1. Select the team you want to create a workspace for. You must have owner or moderator permissions to create a workspace.
    <Image
       className=" rounded-lg border border-gray-100"
       src="https://mintlify.s3-us-west-1.amazonaws.com/orgnise/images/team/teams-list.png"
       alt="List of teams"
       width="1200"
       height="630"
     />
   
2. Click on the **Create Workspace** button
    <Image
       className=" rounded-lg border border-gray-100"
       src="https://mintlify.s3-us-west-1.amazonaws.com/orgnise/images/team/create-workspace-button.png"
       alt="create workspace button"
       width="1200"
       height="630"
     />
   
3. Enter the **Workspace Name**
4. Enter the **Workspace Description**
    <Image
       className=" rounded-lg border border-gray-100"
       src="https://mintlify.s3-us-west-1.amazonaws.com/orgnise/images/team/create-workspace-option.png"
       alt="create workspace model"
       width="1200"
       height="630"
     />
