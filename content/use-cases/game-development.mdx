1. **Asset Management:**

   - Centralizes all game assets, including art, sound, code, and design documents, making it easier for team members to find and use them.

2. **Version Control:**

   - Keeps track of different versions of game assets and code, ensuring that team members are always working with the most current versions.

3. **Bug Tracking:**

   - Stores bug reports and resolution steps, helping the team to quickly identify and fix issues.

4. **Knowledge Sharing:**

   - Facilitates the sharing of best practices, coding standards, and design guidelines, which can improve the overall quality of the game.
