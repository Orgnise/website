---

1. **Centralized Information:**

   - Houses all product-related information, such as feature specifications, user stories, and market research, in one accessible location.

2. **Improved Decision-Making:**

   - Provides access to historical data and analytics, enabling more informed decisions regarding product development and enhancements.

3. **Streamlined Communication:**

   - Facilitates better communication between the product team and other departments like engineering, marketing, and sales by providing a single source of truth.

4. **Faster Onboarding:**

   - New product managers can quickly get up to speed by accessing documented product roadmaps, strategies, and key metrics.
