# Customer Support
A company's customer support team receives a high volume of repetitive questions from customers. 
To reduce the workload and improve response times, the team needs a knowledge base to store and organize answers to frequently asked questions (FAQs). 

Orgnise allows customers support team member to search for answers to common questions, reducing the number of support tickets and enabling support agents to focus on more complex issues

**Benefits**
- Reduced support tickets by 30%
- Improved first-call resolution rate by 25%
- Enhanced customer satisfaction through faster response times
