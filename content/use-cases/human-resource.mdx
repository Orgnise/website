---
title: Human Resource
summary: Capture, organize, and share HR policies, procedures, and training materials in a centralized knowledge base.
---

1. **Policy and Procedure Accessibility:**

   - Provides easy access to company policies, procedures, and employee handbooks, ensuring that all employees are aware of company guidelines.

2. **Training and Development:**

   - Centralizes training materials and development programs, making it easier for employees to access and complete required training.

3. **Employee Self-Service:**

   - Reduces the administrative burden on HR by enabling employees to find answers to common questions about benefits, time-off policies, and other HR-related queries.

4. **Compliance:**

   - Ensures that all HR documentation is up-to-date and compliant with relevant laws and regulations, reducing legal risks.
