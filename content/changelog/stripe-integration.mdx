We are excited to announce a major enhancement to our platform with the integration of the Stripe payment gateway. This update is designed to streamline the subscription and billing process, making it easier than ever to manage your team's plan. Here are the key features included in this update:

1. **Displaying Available Upgrade Plans**
   - Choosing the right subscription plan for your team is now simpler. You can view all available upgrade plans directly on the team billing page. This feature allows you to compare different plans and select the one that best fits your team’s needs.
2. **Plan Upgrade Functionality**
   - Upgrading your subscription plan has never been easier. With our new integration, you can seamlessly upgrade to a higher plan using Stripe. Once you select your desired plan, the upgrade process will be handled smoothly and efficiently.
3. **Email Notifications for Plan Upgrades**
   - To ensure that team owners are always in the loop, we have added a functionality that sends an email notification whenever a plan is upgraded. This keeps team owners informed about any changes to their subscription status.
4. **Manage Subscription Option**
   - Managing your subscription is now more convenient. We have introduced a new option on the team billing page that allows you to modify or cancel your subscription as needed. This provides easy access to manage your subscription without any hassle.
5. **Resource Usage and Limits Display**
   - Understanding your resource usage is crucial for efficient team management. We now display resource usage and limits in the billing section of the team settings. This includes detailed information on content creation, workspace usage, and member invitations.

## **Conclusion**

The integration of the Stripe payment gateway marks a significant step towards providing a robust subscription and billing management system. These features are designed to give you more control over your team’s financials and resource management. Stay tuned for more updates and feel free to reach out to our support team if you have any questions.
