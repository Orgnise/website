We are excited to announce a new set of enhancements to our workspace management features. These improvements focus on updating team and workspace descriptions, member management, and permission controls to offer a more streamlined and secure experience.


#### **1. Update Team and Workspace Descriptions**

- **What's New**: Users can now easily update the descriptions for teams and workspaces, providing better context and clarity.

#### **2. Member Management Enhancements**

- **Add/Remove Members**: You can now add or remove members from a workspace with ease.
- **Update Member Roles**: Roles of workspace members can be updated to ensure appropriate access and permissions.

#### **3. Permission Controls**

- **Restrict Workspace Settings Updates**: Updates to workspace settings are now restricted based on user roles, ensuring that only authorized personnel can make changes.
- **Role-Based Operation Limits**: Users with a "reader" role are now limited in their ability to create, update, or delete items, enhancing security and data integrity.

#### **4. Improved User Visibility**

- **Membership-Based Workspace Visibility**: Users will now only see workspaces they are members of, reducing clutter and improving navigation.

#### **5. User Interface Enhancement**

- **Theme Switcher Relocation**: The theme switcher has been moved to the navigation dropdown for easier access, enhancing user experience.

## **Purpose**

These changes are designed to enhance the overall workspace management experience and provide more granular control over permissions and access. By implementing these updates, we aim to improve the efficiency, security, and usability of our platform.

Stay tuned for more updates as we continue to enhance our system based on your valuable feedback
